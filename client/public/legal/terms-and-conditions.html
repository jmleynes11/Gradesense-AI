GradeSense AI

Terms and Conditions

Last updated: 27 October 2026

These terms govern your use of GradeSense AI for teaching, assessment, grading, feedback, progress tracking, and AI-assisted quiz generation. They apply to Teachers, Students, and Administrators using the system through their school or organization.

1. Accounts and authorized use

Provide accurate account information, including your name, email address, role, and student number when required. Use only the account and permissions assigned to you. Do not impersonate another person, register under a false role, share accounts, or access another user's records without authorization.

Students must follow their school's account authorization arrangements. Where parent or guardian authorization is required, the school must arrange it before the student uses the service. Administrators manage accounts under their institution's procedures.

2. Teacher and Student responsibilities

Teachers are responsible for the assessments, instructions, rubrics, answer keys, deadlines, feedback, and grades they publish or approve. Check generated questions for accuracy, relevance, bias, and suitability for your students before publishing them.

Students must submit their own work, follow assessment instructions and deadlines, and use outside help or AI tools only as their teacher permits. Do not distribute restricted questions, answer keys, another student's submissions, or confidential school records.

3. Acceptable use and system rules

  • Use the system for authorized educational and administrative activities and follow applicable school rules and Philippine law.
  • Do not falsify submissions, attendance-related activity, grades, feedback, identity information, or academic records.
  • Do not bypass access controls, assessment timers, attempt limits, or restrictions on viewing questions and results.
  • Do not upload malware, unlawful or harassing material, or content that infringes another person's rights.
  • Do not disrupt the service, misuse AI-generation requests, probe other accounts, or extract records beyond your assigned access.

4. Account security

Keep your password and sign-in information confidential. Sign out on shared devices and protect access to downloaded reports and file links. Report suspected account compromise, unauthorized access, or incorrect permissions promptly to your school's GradeSense AI administrator. Never include passwords or other secrets in an assessment answer or uploaded lesson.

5. AI assistance and human review

GradeSense AI can generate questions, evaluate answers against reference material and rubrics, suggest feedback, and flag similarities or writing patterns. These outputs can contain mistakes, omissions, or bias. Similarity and AI-content indicators are signals for review and do not establish misconduct by themselves.

Teachers remain responsible for academic decisions and for reviewing disputed or consequential results. Students may raise concerns about a score, feedback, or an integrity flag with their teacher through the school's review process. AI service availability can affect generation and grading completion.

6. Uploaded materials and educational records

Upload only material you are entitled to use and share for the educational task. Uploading a lesson, question, answer, or supporting file permits the processing needed to provide the requested assessment and feedback functions, including configured AI processing described in the Privacy Policy.

Limit personal information to what the task requires. Respect the confidentiality and intellectual-property rights of students, teachers, the school, and other content owners. Access to a record does not authorize you to publish it or use it for an unrelated purpose.

7. Availability, records, and account restrictions

Maintenance, connection failures, and external AI services can interrupt the system. Report failed submissions or missing results to your teacher or administrator so they can determine the appropriate academic response. Follow the school's procedures for preserving important work and official reports.

Authorized administrators may restrict or disable accounts under school procedures, including for security incidents or misuse. Account deactivation does not itself erase educational records; their handling is described in the Privacy Policy and applicable school retention requirements.

8. Privacy, applicable rules, and questions

Personal information is handled as described in the separate Privacy Policy. These terms do not waive data privacy rights or override mandatory Philippine law. Follow your school's published academic and disciplinary procedures for disputes, and contact its GradeSense AI administrator for account or system concerns.

Changes to these terms will be identified by the updated date on this page. Any additional notice or consent required by law remains necessary.
