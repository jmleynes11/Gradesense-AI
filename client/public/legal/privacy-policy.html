GradeSense AI

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy explains how GradeSense AI handles information for account management, assessment, AI-assisted grading and quiz generation, educational feedback, and progress reporting. In a school deployment, the institution determines which educational records are entered and who is authorized to use them. Contact the institution providing your account for questions about its administration of your records.

1. Information collected

  • Account information: first and last name, email address, password hash, role, student number when applicable, optional profile photo, account status, and agreement-acceptance timestamp.
  • Educational records: subject and enrollment details, lessons and uploaded teaching materials, questions, rubrics, answer keys, assessment settings, and deadlines.
  • Student work and results: assessment answers, essays, code, supporting documents, submission and attempt times, quiz-game results and rankings, grades, feedback, teacher comments, grade adjustments, and similarity or AI-content indicators.
  • Operational information: login and record-update times, notifications, audit actions, IP addresses recorded with audit events, and service diagnostic information.

Information comes from account holders, authorized school personnel, user activity, and the system's assessment and reporting processes. Personal details written inside an answer or uploaded file become part of that content.

2. How information is used

The system uses these records to authenticate users, manage enrollment and access, deliver assessments, generate quizzes, evaluate answers, provide feedback, calculate results, display progress and quiz rankings, issue notifications, and prepare academic reports. Audit and diagnostic records help investigate misuse, resolve errors, and maintain the service.

Similarity checks compare answers within an assessment and may retain matching excerpts and the associated student identity for review. Teachers can use those indicators alongside the underlying work and school academic-integrity procedures.

3. Philippine privacy requirements and lawful processing

Processing must follow Republic Act No. 10173, the Data Privacy Act of 2012, its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and applicable National Privacy Commission issuances. Education information is sensitive personal information. The institution must identify a lawful basis under Section 12 for ordinary personal information and a permitted condition under Section 13 for sensitive records.

Only necessary information may be processed for declared educational purposes. Where consent is required, it must be specific and informed. Agreement to these documents does not authorize unrelated processing or waive statutory rights.

4. AI processing and review of results

When AI functions are used, lesson extracts, assessment questions, reference answers, rubrics, and student answer text may be sent to configured external providers, including Groq and OpenAI. A fallback provider may be used when a request fails. Personal information embedded in that material may be included in the request.

AI outputs can affect suggested scores, feedback, generated questions, and integrity indicators and can be inaccurate or biased. Ask your teacher to explain or review a disputed result. The institution must provide meaningful human review where automated decisions create significant risks to a person's rights.

Provider processing may occur outside the Philippines. Provider retention and use of submitted material depend on the applicable service agreement and settings. The institution is responsible for selecting appropriate processing arrangements and safeguards and for explaining them to users.

5. Access and sharing

Records are available through the system's role-based functions to users and authorized Teachers or Administrators who carry out educational and operational tasks. Quiz leaderboards can display participant names, scores, and rankings to users with access to that feature. Exported reports contain the educational information selected for the report.

Hosting, database, and AI service providers may process information needed to operate their services. The institution may disclose records when authorized for an educational purpose or required by law or a lawful request. Staff handling exports must protect them and limit further disclosure to authorized recipients.

6. Storage and protection

Account and educational records are stored in the application's database. Uploaded lessons, supporting documents, and profile images are stored on the application server. Uploaded files are served through direct file URLs; a recipient of a URL may be able to open or share the file. Include only information needed for the educational task and protect those links.

Passwords are stored as hashes. Signed authentication tokens and role checks protect authenticated application functions. Operational logs can contain request metadata and errors; protected grading diagnostics may include encrypted assessment content when that logging is configured. Hosting location, connection security, backup arrangements, and database encryption depend on the institution's deployment.

The institution must maintain appropriate organizational, physical, and technical safeguards and handle security incidents and any required notifications under applicable privacy rules. Report suspected unauthorized access promptly through your school's official support or privacy contact.

7. Browser storage

GradeSense AI stores an authentication token and account details in browser local storage to maintain sign-in, and uses session storage for the privacy-notice acknowledgement. Signing out removes the stored authentication token and account details. Close your session on shared devices and protect browser access.

8. Retention and deletion

Records should be retained only as needed for their stated purposes, lawful recordkeeping, or legal claims. The institution determines the applicable academic-record, upload, audit-log, and backup retention periods. Request its retention schedule through its official privacy contact. Disabling an account or signing out does not automatically delete its educational records.

9. Your rights and requests

Subject to applicable conditions, you may request information about processing, access to your personal data, correction, objection, erasure or blocking, and data portability. You may withdraw consent for processing based on consent, claim damages where legally available, and complain to the National Privacy Commission.

Send requests to your school's GradeSense AI administrator or Data Protection Officer through the school's official contact channels. Identify the affected account and records without sending your password. The institution may verify your identity and explain any legal limits on a request, including required retention of academic records. Use the school's academic-review process to dispute the substance of a grade.

10. Students and guardians

Schools must explain processing in language suitable for their students and arrange any parent or guardian involvement required by law. Students and guardians can contact the school's privacy officer about authorization, access, and concerns involving a minor's records.

11. Policy updates and official guidance

The updated date identifies this version. Changes in purposes or processing must be accompanied by any notice or consent required by law. The following official resources explain Philippine privacy requirements and complaint procedures:

  • Republic Act No. 10173: Data Privacy Act of 2012
  • National Privacy Commission: data subject rights
  • NPC Advisory No. 2024-04: AI systems processing personal data
  • National Privacy Commission: filing a complaint
